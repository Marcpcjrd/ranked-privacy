Ranked — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Ranked ("the game") is a mobile game developed by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the online leaderboards.

Data we collect

DataWhy
A random anonymous account identifier, created automatically the first time the game goes online To link your scores to your pilot name. It is not linked to your real identity, email or phone number.
The pilot name you choose (3–12 letters/digits)Shown publicly on the leaderboards.
Run results: score, altitude, number of kills, run duration, plane used, game mode and date, game version To build the leaderboards and to detect impossible (cheated) scores.
Technical connection data (such as IP address) processed by our server provider when the game connects Needed to deliver the service and keep it secure. We do not use it to identify you.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising ID or any other personal information. The game contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data stored only on your device

Your best scores, unlocked planes and settings are saved locally on your phone. They never leave the device unless you submit a run to the online leaderboard.

Where the data is stored

Online data is stored by our database provider, Supabase (servers in the European Union), and is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Public information

Your pilot name, your best scores and the plane used are visible to all players on the leaderboards.

How long we keep it

Online data is kept as long as your leaderboard entry exists. You can delete it at any time (see below).

Deleting your data

  • In the game: open Leaderboard and tap Delete my online data twice. Your pilot name, all your scores and your anonymous account are permanently removed from our server.
  • By email: write to gameoftheworldyt@gmail.com with your pilot name and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Uninstalling the game deletes the data stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Permissions used by the app

  • Internet — to send scores and load the leaderboards.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback during gameplay.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
